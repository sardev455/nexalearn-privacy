Privacy policy

Cashier Practice · Version 1.0 · Updated 2026-10-10

Developer and contact

Cashier Practice is provided by nexalearn. For privacy questions or support, email sarfrazlearningsystem@gmail.com.

Offline practice

This version works offline. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics or in-app purchases. The app does not automatically send practice data to the developer or a server, and does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or advertising identifier access.

Data on your device

The app stores practice questions, cash selections, answers, attempts, session times, progress and settings in its local app storage so you can resume and review practice. Session identifiers are generated locally for practice records; they are not device identifiers. Practice amounts are fictional, and the app does not process payments.

Local records stay on your device until removed in settings, by clearing app data, or by uninstalling. App-managed Android backup is disabled. The developer cannot access or remotely delete these local records.

Export and deletion

Export results creates a JSON file only when you request it. You choose the destination in Android's document picker. A cloud storage destination may upload the file under that provider's own privacy policy. No copy is automatically sent to the developer. Exported files contain practice summaries and answers and cannot restore app progress.

Use Your data and settings → Delete all practice data to remove local progress. Exported or shared copies must be deleted separately wherever you saved or sent them.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address and anything you include, and uses them to respond to your request. Do not include payment details or other sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations. You can request its deletion using the contact above, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements. Email and any storage service you choose operate under their own policies.

Security and sharing

Local app data is kept in Android's app storage. This is not a secure vault; protect access to your device and any exported files. The developer does not sell practice data. Information you voluntarily send for support is handled through the email provider and may be disclosed when legally required.

Changes

If app features or data practices change, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated. This policy describes version 1.0, which has no ads.
